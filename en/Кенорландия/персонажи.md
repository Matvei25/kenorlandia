## South Park

Characters from the mountain town of South Park (Oregon, USA)

### Eric
- **Surname**: Cartman
- **Age**: 10 years old
- **Gender**: Boy
- **Character**: Egocentric, sly, manipulative, loud, cowardly, but sometimes brilliant in his devious schemes
- **Interests**: Money, power, fast food, manipulating people, creating his own microstates modeled on Yotagos (but without science, just partying)
- **Skills**: Oratory, blackmail, negotiations with Alliance defectors, selling information to both sides, quick adaptation to chaos
- **Traits**: Wears a blue-and-yellow beanie with a pom-pom and a red jacket, always tries to create his own "Citizen's Defense Committee," which in reality is extortion of farmers and a Yotagos party. Dreams of becoming the leader of all of South Park and striking a deal with the Alliance, but constantly fails. Despises the "nerds" from Technograd (Kenorlandia), though he envies their technology
- **Company**: Citizen's Defense Committee (CDC, `cartmancdc.co`)
- **Languages**: English, a bit of Spanish swearing
- **Favorite color**: Red (like his jacket)
- **Family**: Mother (Lina Cartman) — works at the "At Mary's" bar, father unknown

### Stan
- **Surname**: Marsh
- **Age**: 10 years old
- **Gender**: Boy
- **Character**: Smart, level-headed, a bit cynical, but with a sense of justice
- **Interests**: Protecting his friends, exposing Cartman's schemes, studying Alliance technology in an underground workshop (helps the "Committee of Watchers")
- **Skills**: Analytical thinking, handling simple tools, stealth
- **Traits**: Usually the voice of reason in the group, friends with Kyle and Kenny. His father Randy sometimes gets involved in strange projects with jellies, which annoys Stan
- **Languages**: English
- **Favorite color**: Blue
- **Family**: Father Randy Marsh (geologist, cooperates with local researchers), mother Sharon, sister Shelley

### Kyle
- **Surname**: Broflovski
- **Age**: 10 years old
- **Gender**: Boy
- **Character**: Smart, sarcastic, irritable, but a loyal friend
- **Interests**: Politics, reading, criticizing Cartman, studying languages (Hebrew thanks to his mother)
- **Skills**: Persuasion, analysis, knowledge of history (including the Alliance occupation), communicating with jellies (they respect him)
- **Traits**: Wears a green ushanka. Often argues with Cartman about Alliance and Kenorlandia politics. Maintains contacts with Aredan refugees through his relatives
- **Languages**: English, Hebrew (basic)
- **Favorite color**: Green
- **Family**: Mother Sheila (activist, tries to oppose Alliance policy), father Gerald (lawyer), brother Ike (adopted, from Canada)

### Kenny
- **Surname**: McCormick
- **Age**: 10 years old
- **Gender**: Boy
- **Character**: Kind, brave, resourceful, quiet (his speech is unintelligible, but everyone understands him)
- **Interests**: Games, survival, protecting the weak, odd jobs for local farmers
- **Skills**: Stealth, knife-throwing (picks up work at livestock slaughter), knowledge of dead languages (due to frequent "rebirths")
- **Traits**: **Immortal** — after every death (and he dies almost every day from run-ins with antlions or Alliance soldiers) he comes back to life the next morning, but nobody except his friends notices it. He keeps this ability secret from the Alliance, fearing they'll take him for experiments. He adores playing with jellies and helps them build shelters
- **Languages**: English (unintelligible)
- **Favorite color**: Orange (like his parka)
- **Family**: Father Stuart (alcoholic, works at a sawmill), mother Carol (unemployed), brother Kevin

### Butters
- **Surname**: Stotch
- **Age**: 10 years old
- **Gender**: Boy
- **Character**: Naive, trusting, kind, often becomes a victim of Cartman's pranks, but in critical situations shows unexpected cleverness
- **Interests**: Playing with friends, collecting bugs (including antlions, which he tries to tame), watching jellies
- **Skills**: Surveillance, moving unnoticed, knowledge of local creatures' habits, the ability to calm animals and semi-intelligent beings (the kolbasites leave him alone because he isn't afraid of them)
- **Traits**: Always wears an orange jacket and blue pants. His parents constantly punish him, but he still sticks his nose everywhere. Because of his trustfulness he often falls into Alliance traps, but Kenny saves him or the jellies intervene unexpectedly. Secretly dreams of creating his own microstate, "Buttersland," but tells nobody about it
- **Languages**: English
- **Favorite color**: Orange (like his jacket)
- **Family**: Father Stephen Stotch (strict, works at an insurance company), mother Linda (housewife)
