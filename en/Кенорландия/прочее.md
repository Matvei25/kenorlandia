### Magnetostation
A station where electricity is extracted from magnets by spinning (rivers, labor or wind).

### UGST
In some cities there are **sausage districts** — these are urban-type districts where kolbasites live, in small houses, but with restrictions: a fence, a checkpoint, autonomy like the UGST (Urban Great Sausage Tribes). The majority of sausages in the wild are in the GST (83%), the rest (17%) in the Arab districts. There are highways that lead to GST settlements/other UGSTs and often break off, replaced by roads or paths. There are ordinary not-wide roads in UGSTs. Kolbasites there use Arbsos.

There is a domain zone: `.gst`; ISO: `gst`; phone code +018

### Transport
- Cars — carts with a roof, four wheels, five seats, a hood and a trunk, resembling automobiles, powered by magnets, a pedal and a steering wheel
- Buses
- Semi trucks
- Rafts
- Gliders
- Golf carts

They use them to quickly get to another place without spending effort; they are used for civic, military, invasion and public purposes.

### Weapons
Kolbasites also have their own military power, which is built up in garrisons and barracks; kolbasites are trained there in how to fight, brawl and bite effectively, and are mobilized

## Jellies

### Cities

Jellies live in human cities and are civilians. They can also build their own cities, houses and slums!

### Internet

They have their own top-level domain: `.jel`; ISO: `jel`; as well as phone code +035

## Linuxoids

## Monkekhi

### Cities

Monkekhi live predominantly in tropical forests, building cities in the trees and in the canopies. In human cities they form small enclaves, often in parks or abandoned buildings. They are civilians.

### Internet

They have their own top-level domain: `.mok`; ISO: `mok`; as well as phone code +066. The quality of the internet in forest cities leaves much to be desired due to communication interruptions.

### Weapons

Monkekhi use primitive weapons: slings, bows and banana launchers, but also actively employ captured firearms obtained from other races. Their infantry is very mobile and moves excellently through the trees.

## Real computer viruses
Not excluded from the lore.

## The Yobob virus

A virus that spreads through download links, messengers and Email, infects Android and mobile Linux systems; it:
- Opens websites
- Creates dialog boxes
- Opens an overlay
- Opens ads
- Asks for money
- Asks for `root`
- Opens an overlay with random content from various sites
- Shows screamers
- Looks for APIs to confidential data and to banking apps

Android — `com.yobob.bankerllc`
Linux — `yobob-bank-app`

## The père zapin virus

A virus with a French package name that spreads on Android. On the home screen its icon looks like a Trollge with black blood pouring from its empty eyes. It has the name "تین نسیم حسن حسن père zapin". Appearance-wise it looks like an empty app:
- A large caption "और इस प्रकार हम आप लोग इस तरह चीन व थांग इंग और इस तरह हम इस पर कोई" in Hindi
- An Android icon below

Its simple look encourages the user to delete the virus. This virus sends data to whoever suggested downloading it. But upon deletion a script triggers that wipes everything it has access to.

Android — `com.pere.zapin.india`

This is the SI, but with additional axioms:

- Steradian sr = 1 rad²
- Binary prefixes -bi-
- Natural prefixes -na-

## Theory of Semi-Intelligence
The theory of semi-intelligence states that semi-intelligent intellect and a neural network can be predicted with high or full accuracy. It was discovered and confirmed at EBM in 2024, which made it possible to predict behavior and model the invasions of sausages and Durachochki. The behavior of semi-intelligent intellects is comparable to neural networks.
