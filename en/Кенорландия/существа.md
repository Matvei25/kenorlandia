# Creatures

Sentient creatures of the universe.

### Humans, animals and advisors

They're on the list, but it's too trivial to describe them here, since they've been described in another file or need no introduction.

## Jellies

- **Appearance**: Jelly creatures with little antennae on their head, a sole instead of feet, eyes, a mouth, tiny nostrils, paws. They look like a lump, a drop or a blob. The antennae are their organs of hearing
- **Color**: They have their own individual unchanging colors
- **Average lifespan**: ~62 years
- **Temperament**: Friendly to everyone except kolbasites
- **Mind**: Sentient, they can talk, think, solve and build, they have names
- **Construction**: They build from materials that can be used for construction. They build settlements near cities, rivers, shores and mountains
- **Habitat**: In jelly settlements and in human settlements
- **Abilities**: They have no supernatural abilities, but they're cute and curious
- **Locomotion**: They crawl on their sole
- **Range**: Everywhere except arctic deserts and tundras
- **Intelligence**: Like humans, but a bit lower, they can make technology

## Kolbasites

- **Appearance**: Living sausages, often with red spots
- **Size**: 5-25 cm
- **Average lifespan**: ~15 years
- **Languages**: They speak Semitic, Iranian, Abkhazo-Adyghean, Turkic, African, Kenorian and Kolbasite creole (their own group of creole languages) languages (they don't speak any others)
- **Temperament**: Insane, they bite, scream, twitch, mostly hostile to everyone except headcrabs; they love to cram excessive punctuation and symbols into writing and to mix languages
- **Mind**: Semi-sentient, they have names
- **Locomotion**: They have no limbs, they roll and jump
- **Habitat**: The Middle East, the Central East, North Africa, the Balkans, the Caucasus, Transcaucasia
- **Diet**: Grass, but they can bite animals and people
- **Reproduction**: Sexually
- **Features**: Eyeless, blind, afraid of nothing
- **Construction**: They can build shelters from cardboard, wood, stone, plastic, scrap metal, wires and penoplex
- **Writing**: They can write and read (using engraving, charcoal, etc.)
- **Handwriting**: Barely legible to humans, but they themselves read it very well
- **Behavior**: They often stage invasions

## Antlions

- **Average lifespan**: ~8 years
- **Size**: 0.5–1 m
- **Features**: An eyeless bug, it burrows into the sand, waits for prey, fairly fast, has beetle wings
- **Fears**: Vibrations (from the Alliance's thumpers/diggers)
- **Bait**: Feropods (fruits whose juice attracts the bugs)
- **Armor**: Their beetle armor is flimsy, you can punch through it with a crowbar
- **Taming**: Just take a feropod in your hands and smear its juice from your palm to your elbow

### Feropods
Fruit-baits for bugs. Tastes like rotten potato

## Vortigaunts

- **Appearance**: Green skin, a head on a long neck pointing forward, one big red eye, two arms from the shoulders and one on the chest, heavy feet that make jumping impossible
- **Average lifespan**: ~86 years
- **Size**: Human-sized
- **Behavior**: Friendly toward humans and jellies
- **Capabilities**: On par with human ones
- **Languages**: All human languages and Vortigaunt
- **Mind**: Sentient, they have names
- **Reproduction**: Sexually
- **Features**: They can shoot a glowing green liquid that can both heal and maim — it's all vortal abilities. They glow violet with their body, and blue with their eyes

## Durachochki

- **Size**: About 0.8 m
- **Average lifespan**: ~45 years
- **Type**: Humanoid, bipedal creatures
- **Intelligence**: Semi-sentient
- **Capabilities**: They can do everything jellies and sausages can
- **Behavior**: They run around like mad, build settlements, stage invasions
- **Dream**: To create the state of Turaduna all over the world

## Linuxoids

- **Appearance**: A mix of the average look of a little penguin and an emperor penguin, i.e. it looks like Tux
- **Average lifespan**: ~57 years
- **Temperament**: Friendly to everyone except kolbasites
- **Mind**: Sentient, they have names
- **Locomotion**: On two legs, on their belly over the ice, with their flippers in the water
- **Habitat**: Arctic deserts, but rarely elsewhere
- **Intelligence**: Like humans
- **Features**: More agile than penguins, they have teeth, they build settlements on the shores of arctic deserts or closer to the poles on any terrain

## Headcrabs

- **Appearance**: Sac-like creatures with four pointed limbs. They move on their hind legs and small front paws, or on all four. On the underside of the body is a wide sucker-like mouth opening with sharp hook-teeth along the edges. The skin is off-white with a yellowish tint, the legs are colored blood-red and black. They have two formations on the front of the body resembling spider chelicerae with hook-shaped teeth. Eyes are absent.
- **Size**: Body length from the front limbs to the back ones — about 60 cm. Comparable to a turkey carcass or a watermelon.
- **Average lifespan**: ~5–7 years (in the wild), they can live longer in laboratory conditions.
- **Temperament**: Aggressive parasites, they hunt stealthily, attack from ambushes. In groups they display reckless bravery, chasing prey even at the cost of their kin's death. They squeak and make other sounds.
- **Mind**: Semi-sentient, they act mostly on instinct.
- **Locomotion**: On four legs, they can burrow into the ground. They can leap distances of up to 3–10 meters.
- **Habitat**: Dark corners, ventilation shafts, caves, ruins. They are found in cities, wastelands, laboratories. They can't swim and drown in water.
- **Diet**: Omnivorous parasites. Their main way of feeding is parasitizing humans and transhumans. Vortigaunts and humans eat them as food.
- **Reproduction**: They are born from a gonarch (a giant headcrab queen). Some headcrabs can turn into gonarchs over time.
- **Features**: When attacking they leap onto the victim's head, pierce the neck with a stinger and connect to the spinal cord. The victim turns into a zombie — a puppet whose body mutates. The host cannot be saved. They are resistant to toxic waste and radioactive contamination.
- **Natural enemies**: Humans and vortigaunts.

### Known species

- **Common headcrab** — the classic species, found everywhere. Turns victims into ordinary zombies.
- **Fast headcrab** — a faster and more aggressive variety, presumably arising from adaptation to urban conditions.
- **Poison headcrab** — distinguished by a poisonous bite dangerous to human life.

### Interaction with the Alliance

The Alliance calls headcrabs «parasitics» and «biotics». Alliance scientists studied headcrabs and, according to some accounts, took part in their adaptation to Earth's conditions, creating new species. Headcrabs remain one of the most numerous threats in the territory controlled by the Alliance, and pose a danger to both civilians and soldiers.

## Monkehs

- **Appearance**: A mix of the average look of a gorilla and a macaque without legs, three visible fingers (the other two fingers were modified), like a player from Gorilla Tag VR
- **Color**: Their fur has its own individual unchanging color, the skin is pale beige
- **Average lifespan**: About ~42 years
- **Temperament**: Neutral
- **Mind**: Sentient, they can talk, think, solve and build, they have names
- **Locomotion**: On their hands
- **Diet**: Omnivorous
- **Reproduction**: Sexually
- **Features**: Very fast, short reaction time, they love to play
- **Intelligence**: A bit dumber than humans, they can make technology

> Sentient individuals can have a spread of intelligence levels.
