# Yotagos

Matvey created his own microstate for friends called Yotagos (a large-scale kids' project, founded in 2023, means nothing to Kenorlandia) 2 km from Sochi:
- **Yota Department** — the main building, called the Yotacenter, which houses:
- - **Yotahotel** — a dormitory for friends and other neighborhood kids, several small rooms, with a basement, an attic (dance floor), a cellar (sausage gasoline), a rec room
- - **Servers** — a server hall, located deep in the utility rooms
- - **Lupa-and-Pupa** — a bar and snack bar
- - **Yotaoffice** — an office, a co-working space with old phones (they run the best LineageOS software combos), VR headsets, laptops, microcontrollers; locals in Yotagos and neighborhood kids work here
- - **Utility rooms** — corridors through which you can reach every part of the Yotacenter and the secret rooms, plus four punishment cells, one of which leads to the rec room
- **Parliament of the Yotian State** — a building made of construction materials and stone; parliamentarians work here, and the rulers settle state matters here
- **Forces of the Yotian State** — a building called the Yotaforces, which holds weapons, firecrackers, airguns and rubber-bullet guns for the war with the GST and Turaduna; a few more neighborhood kids work there
- **Teta Department** — a building called the Tetacenter, founded by Netur; his friends and other neighborhood kids live here, there are two punishment cells
- **"Mirzoyan" Warehouse** — a building storing everything that didn't fit in the Yotacenter and the Tetacenter
- **The Agency** — a building housing the Agency for Finding New Premises for Yotagos, the Candidate-Resident Agency
- **Pam Department** — a "building" called the Pemicenter, made of cardboard and foam insulation, roughly the same as the Yotacenter on the inside
- **Gamma Department** — a building called the Gammacenter, made of construction materials; Tuks's friends and other neighborhood kids live here, there are three punishment cells
- **Yota-TV** — a micro-building made of construction materials; it has a server that runs TV over the shared Wi-Fi and ESP-NOW, with a chat, site `localhost:303`, also redirected to `yotastate.kl/tv-live`
- **Ballist-1** and **Ballist-2** — micro-buildings built of construction materials; they house launchers and several Yotian ballistic rockets (YBR), with firecrackers and any filler for damage inside
- **Accounting** — a micro-building built of construction materials
- **"Yotian" Airfield** — a building called the Airport, erected from construction materials; it has a 50 m runway, a tower and a hall
- **Laboratory** — a building called the Univerlab, built of construction materials and foam insulation with reinforced ventilation. Experimental substances are stored and studied there
- **"Tsentik" Shop** — a building made of construction materials; food, water, Kenorian ELJs (in exchange for goods) and souvenirs are sold here
- **The Rostrum** — a building made of construction materials; people perform and give speeches here
- **"Simersky" Office** — a building made of construction materials; people work at computers here
- **"Akhmediy" Factory** — a building made of construction materials, foam insulation and scrap metal; technologies are produced here
- **"F-Sharp" Factory** — a building made of construction materials, foam insulation and scrap metal
- **"Doctor" Medical Station** — a small building made of construction materials, first-aid kit, disinfection, dressing room
- **"Proton" Shopping Gallery** — a building made of construction materials and scrap metal; it houses 4 shops
- **Farm** — a 0.3 km² field; vegetables, fruits, berries are grown here
- Several structures, warehouses, factories, huts, garden beds and micro-buildings out of cardboard, construction materials, scrap metal and foam insulation
- Houses with little apartments

> Yotagos's buildings stand next to each other.
> Yotagos is 32 km from Mazyam and 17 km from Al-Abkhaz.
> The YBR launcher has a propane compressor, an igniter, a guide rail and an onboard computer. YBR range ≈ 40 km.
> All over Yotagos there are Wi-Fi, RSSI and ESP-NOW beacons, public cameras, sirens on piezo speakers.
> Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Orange Pi, ESP and STM play a role in the electronic devices of Matvey and Tuks.
