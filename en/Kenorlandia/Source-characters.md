## Source games characters

### Half-Life 2

#### Gordon
- **Surname**: Freeman
- **Age**: 27 years old (biological)
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Silent, determined, persistent, smart, calm, but acts tough and uncompromising in critical situations
- **Interests**: Physics, quantum mechanics, survival, protecting people
- **Skills**: Handling various weapons (from a crowbar to a pulse rifle), hacking Combine systems, driving vehicles, tactical thinking, high-energy physics
- **Gear**: HEV suit (Hazardous Environment Suit) with a life-support system, crowbar, gravity gun (Zero Point Energy Field Manipulator)
- **Languages**: English, Kenorian, a bit of Russian (understands)
- **Traits**: Legend of the Resistance, "The One Free Man." After the Black Mesa incident he was put into stasis by G-Man and returned 20 years later. A man of few words, but his actions speak louder. Always wears an orange protective suit and glasses. Considered a symbol of hope for those oppressed by the Combine.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance and EBM
- **Nickname**: "Freeman", "The One Free Man"
- **Favorite color**: Orange (like his suit)
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: +725 (500) 000 00-01

#### Alyx
- **Surname**: Vance
- **Age**: 24 years old
- **Gender**: Female
- **Character**: Energetic, smart, curious, brave, emotional, loyal to friends and the cause of the Resistance
- **Interests**: Robotics, hacking, weapons, adventure, music (plays guitar)
- **Skills**: Hacking, handling electronics, sniper shooting, piloting, repairing and modifying tech, knowledge of Combine languages
- **Gear**: Submachine gun, shotgun, hacking tool, homemade devices, robot dog Dog
- **Languages**: English, a bit of Russian, Kenorian, Lojban
- **Traits**: Daughter of Eli Vance. Grew up under occupation, helped her father since childhood. Wears practical clothes with Resistance symbols. Has a sharp mind and learns quickly. Her best friend is the robot Dog.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance and EBM
- **Nickname**: "Alyx"
- **Favorite color**: Blue
- **Family**: Father — Eli Vance, mother died during the Combine invasion
- **Phone number**: +725 (500) 685 62-68

#### Barney
- **Surname**: Calhoun
- **Age**: 41 years old
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Friendly, resourceful, brave, with a sense of humor, but can be serious in dangerous situations
- **Interests**: Survival, undercover work, helping people
- **Skills**: Reconnaissance, shooting, disguise, knowledge of Civil Protection procedures, ability to obtain information
- **Gear**: Pistol, Civil Protection uniform (undercover), radio
- **Languages**: English, a bit of Russian
- **Traits**: Former Black Mesa security guard, during the Combine invasion was forced to work for them undercover. Infiltrated Civil Protection, passes information to the Resistance. Risks his life every day. Once helped Gordon Freeman escape.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance (embedded in Combine Civil Protection)
- **Nickname**: "Barney"
- **Favorite color**: Green
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: +0 (077) 555 12-34 and +725 (500) 998 51-05

#### Eli
- **Surname**: Vance
- **Age**: 60 years old
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Wise, calm, level-headed, caring father, leader willing to take risks for others
- **Interests**: Science, Resistance, family, restoring technology
- **Skills**: Physicist, engineer, organizer, strategist, knowledge of Combine and Black Mesa technology
- **Gear**: Laboratory equipment, teleporter, communications
- **Languages**: English, a bit of Russian, Kenorian (understands)
- **Traits**: One of the founders of the Resistance. Worked at Black Mesa before the incident. Lost his wife during the Combine invasion. His main goal is to free Earth and protect his daughter. Respected by everyone in the Resistance.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance and EBM
- **Nickname**: "Dr. Vance"
- **Favorite color**: Gray
- **Family**: Daughter — Alyx Vance, wife deceased
- **Phone number**: +725 (500) 001 58-88

#### Isaac
- **Surname**: Kleiner
- **Age**: 57 years old
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Absent-minded, brilliant, kind, a bit eccentric, devoted to science and friends
- **Interests**: Quantum physics, teleportation, experiments, Vortigaunts
- **Skills**: Theoretical physicist, engineer, work with teleporters, knowledge of xenobiology
- **Gear**: Laboratory equipment, teleporter, devices for studying Vortigaunts
- **Languages**: English, Russian (poor), Vortigaunt language (partially)
- **Traits**: Former colleague of Gordon Freeman at Black Mesa. After the incident became one of the key scientists of the Resistance. Lives in an abandoned lab with the Vortigaunt Lamarr (a tame headcrab pet). A bit quirky, but his knowledge is invaluable.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance and EBM
- **Nickname**: "Dr. Kleiner"
- **Favorite color**: White
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: +725 (500) 985 04-12

#### Judith
- **Surname**: Mossman
- **Age**: 40 years old
- **Gender**: Female
- **Character**: Secretive, smart, ambitious, dual nature, but ultimately chooses the right side
- **Interests**: Science, power, survival, Combine technology
- **Skills**: Physicist, work with teleporters, espionage, data analysis
- **Gear**: Laboratory equipment, communications, pistol
- **Languages**: English, Russian (poor)
- **Traits**: Worked for the Combine but secretly helped the Resistance. Her motives were unclear for a long time. At a critical moment she helped Alyx and Gordon. Wears glasses and strict clothing. A complex personality seeking redemption.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance, The Combine Empire (formerly) and EBM
- **Nickname**: "Judith"
- **Favorite color**: Purple
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: +0 (077) 555 67-89 and +725 (500) 754 28-96

#### Father Grigori
- **Surname**: Unknown
- **Age**: 70 years old
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Kind, religious, brave, ready to help anyone, a bit eccentric
- **Interests**: Faith, helping people, hunting headcrabs, protecting the weak
- **Skills**: Shotgun handling, knowledge of headcrab habits, medicine (first aid), ability to calm people
- **Gear**: Shotgun, religious attributes, first-aid kit
- **Languages**: Russian, English (poor)
- **Traits**: Lives in an abandoned church in Zombie City (Ravenholm). Considers headcrabs "demons" but still helps survivors. His sermons are full of hope. Wears a tattered cassock and is always ready to fight back.
- **Nickname**: "Father Grigori"
- **Favorite color**: Brown
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: None

#### G-Man
- **Surname**: Unknown
- **Age**: Unknown (looks like a middle-aged man)
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Mysterious, imperturbable, always calm, with an almost indifferent demeanor. Speaks slowly, with pauses and strange intonations. His true motives are unknown, so it's impossible to say which side he's on. However, he clearly opposes the Combine.
- **Interests**: Unknown. Presumably carries out the orders of his employers, using useful people and creatures for some purpose.
- **Skills**: Invulnerability to conventional weapons, interspatial teleportation, moving other beings between spaces, manipulating objects, holding people in stasis.
- **Gear**: Unknown. Always carries a briefcase whose contents are never revealed.
- **Languages**: English, with a distinctive manner of speech and accent.
- **Traits**: Stays in the shadows, observes key events and intervenes only when necessary. Uses Gordon Freeman as a tool against the Combine, but his own plans remain a mystery.
- **Company**: Unknown employers
- **Nickname**: "G-Man", "The Man in the Suit"
- **Favorite color**: Gray
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: Unknown, but perhaps there is a push-button phone in the briefcase

#### Uriah
- **Surname**: Unknown
- **Species**: Vortigaunt
- **Age**: Unknown
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Devoted to science, respectful, focused, tries to stay inconspicuous but speaks directly when necessary
- **Interests**: Science, research, helping Arne Magnusson, working with strider-killing devices
- **Skills**: Scientific research, working with complex White Forest systems, access to secure compartments, teaching others to use Magnusson devices
- **Gear**: White Forest laboratory equipment, Magnusson devices
- **Languages**: Vortigaunt, English
- **Traits**: Wears a white lab coat. One of the few Vortigaunts called by name. Helps Magnusson and opens access to the second compartment for Gordon. Treats humans calmly and shares the goals of the Resistance.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance and EBM
- **Nickname**: "Uriah"
- **Favorite color**: White
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: None

#### Arne
- **Surname**: Magnusson
- **Age**: 60 years old
- **Gender**: Male
- **Character**: Grumpy, sarcastic, arrogant, brilliant, holds grudges (especially against Gordon Freeman for the microwave casserole incident), but ultimately loyal to the Resistance and dedicated to science
- **Interests**: Physics, engineering, weapons development, proving his superiority over Kleiner and Vance, perfecting the Magnusson Device
- **Skills**: Physicist, engineer, inventor, expert in Combine technology, creator of the Magnusson Device (Strider Buster), talented orator (when he wants to be)
- **Gear**: White Forest laboratory equipment, Magnusson Devices, a clipboard, a permanent scowl
- **Languages**: English, a bit of Russian, Kenorian (understands)
- **Traits**: Former colleague of Kleiner and Eli Vance at Black Mesa. Famous for the microwave casserole incident, which he never lets Gordon forget. Runs the White Forest base with an iron fist. Despite his abrasive personality, he is respected for his genius and dedication. Works closely with Uriah and the Vortigaunts. Believes in rigorous testing and preparation before combat.
- **Company**: Lambda Resistance, EBM and White Forest
- **Nickname**: "Dr. Magnusson", "Arne"
- **Favorite color**: White (lab coat) or red (when angry)
- **Family**: Unknown
- **Phone number**: +725 (500) 452 18-73

### Portal

#### Chell
- **Surname**: Unknown
- **Age**: 39 years old (biological)
- **Gender**: Female
- **Character**: Silent, persistent, inventive, with incredible willpower, sarcastic (internally) but outwardly calm
- **Interests**: Survival, solving puzzles, freedom, sometimes revenge on GLaDOS
- **Skills**: Handling the portal gun, solving complex physics puzzles, jumping, coordination, partial resistance to neurotoxin
- **Gear**: Portal gun (Aperture Science Handheld Portal Device), orange jumpsuit, long spring boots
- **Languages**: English (understands but does not speak), possibly others (unknown)
- **Traits**: The only survivor of GLaDOS's tests. Doesn't say a word, but her actions speak for themselves. Has a unique ability to pass any Aperture Science test. Eventually destroyed GLaDOS (temporarily) and escaped. Later was returned for new tests. Now free in Kenorlandia. Her story is a symbol of the fight against a mad AI.
- **Company**: Aperture Science (tester, involuntarily) and EBM
- **Nickname**: "Chell", "Test Subject"
- **Favorite color**: Orange (like her jumpsuit)
- **Family**: Unknown (possibly the daughter of an Aperture scientist)
- **Phone number**: None
