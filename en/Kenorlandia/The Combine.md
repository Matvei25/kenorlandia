# The Combine

A multiworld empire that has conquered several hundred systems.

## Flag, coat of arms and anthem

Flag:
- A dark-blue field
- A black stripe on the left
- A vertical inscription on the stripe, in a geometric font, "CMB"
- A solid gold circle set inside an open gold ring with sharp ends, tilted up-left

Coat of arms — the same circle and ring, in gold

Anthem — "Our Overwatch expanse"

## Structure

- **The Citadel** — the main base (about 5 km tall)
- **"Nova-Prospekt" Prison** — a place where people are processed
- **Industry** — handles the creation of technology, the creation of implants for synths and the extraction of various materials (water, oil, resin, plant seeds, acids, alkalis, gases, rock and ore)
- **Parties**: The Combine Empire, The Combine Overwatch, The Combine Diplomacy
- **Currency**: Credits (CMBCRD) or points (CMBPNT)
- **Language:** English and Lojban

## Units

### Citizens
- Combine civilians
- Jellies
- Lesser advisors
- **Durachochki**

### Security
- CP (Civil Protection)
- **Kolbasites** — creatures used as security/bio-weapons
- **Antlions** — creatures that are used as security/bio-weapons in desert biomes

### Soldiers (the main combat unit)
- Soldiers, snipers, infantrymen, heavy infantrymen
- Biogenerals (they command the sausages)
- **Durachochki** — creatures that are used as micro-soldiers

### Elite
- Shotgun soldiers, grenadiers
- Elite soldiers (with AV2), generals

### Slaves
- Stalkers — reprocessed people with a laser device at their eyes
- **Durachochki** — are also slaves

### Synths
- **Striders** — tripods about 10 m tall, they have an AV3 machine gun and an annihilation cannon
- **Hunters** — tripods 2.5 m tall, they have two blue glowing eyes stacked vertically and a tongue for attacking
- **Dropship** — a flying creature for hauling cargo, 7 m long, it can drag containers for infantry, striders (curled into a ball), APCs and containers for drones/mines; it has 6 legs for grabbing and 2 legs for engines
- **Gunship** — an insect-like creature with an AV1 machine gun (7 m long)
- **Crab synth** — a crustacean synth 2 m tall, it fires firearms
- **Mortar** — a synth hovering 0.2 m above the ground, it fires grenades
- **Advisor** — a larva-like synth with technology for manipulating space-time and the psyche (chemically), 3 m long and 1 m wide/tall; they are the leaders, average lifespan of ~140 years

### Thumpers/diggers
A tall structure (10 m), whose job is to stand on the sands and thump.

### Scanner drone
A drone with a camera with a bright flash, two Combine batteries, a red eyepiece, levitating engines and a mechanical tail

### Roller mine
A spherical mine that rolls along the ground to the enemy, accumulates a charge and extends to strike with electricity; it is also hydrophobic (it explodes on contact with water)

### Anti-personnel hopper mine
A mine that anchors to the ground with three legs; when it detects an enemy nearby it detaches, jumps at the opponent and explodes; it has an indicator

### Manhack
A portable drone with a saw held horizontally in its core; it has an LED that glows depending on its state; CP members carry 1-2 of them on their back in a closed state

### Helicopter
A helicopter that can fire rockets, ball road bombs and from an AV3 cannon. It carries one pilot and two passengers.

### APC
An armored vehicle with an electric motor, 4.2 m long and 2 m tall; they have a stationary AV3 machine gun, a rocket launcher and bulletproof glass

### Indicators
They run on 12 V, they can glow green, yellow, red, blue and white.

### Batteries
Combine batteries, about 8 cm in size; one is enough for 3 weeks of continuous operation. They are inserted into a device like a bottle cork.

### Barricades
They create panels from a super-solid material that crystallizes in 45°/90° shapes, blurs objects behind it and is quite strong

### Energy node
A device with two nodes that absorb energy from energy orbs

### Energy cable
A device with two nodes that conduct energy and energy orbs in both directions

### Charger
A panel with a button and a port for charging Combine devices and their batteries, it emits an orange glow

### Medkit panel
A panel with a button, a bar and an emitter for dispensing healing material; the bar shows the balance of medicine in it; it emits an azure color

## Enemies
