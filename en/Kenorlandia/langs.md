# Languages

Dictionaries of the fictional languages of Kurlyka's universe.

## The Kenorian language

**Origin:** A mix of Russian, English and Arabic. It formed in Kenorlandia as a result of cultural mixing after the creation of the USSR.

**Family:** It stands in its own family of Kenorian languages

**Endonym:** *Kenoris* (Kenorian).

**Writing system:** Latin. There is no fixed letter order; a standard Latin alphabet is used with the addition of digraphs (kh, sh, ch, th, gh) for Arabic sounds.

**Features:** A simple grammatical structure, no genders and no cases (except the possessive). Verbs are not conjugated by person; tense is conveyed with auxiliary words. Word order is SVO (subject-verb-object). The key word *Progrevti* (progress) is used in the name of the state and the ideology.

## The Aredan language

**Origin:** A mix of English, Hebrew and Abkhaz. Widespread in the Aredan Republic; it arose after the war with the Combine as a means of communication between scattered communities.

**Family:** It belongs to the Abkhazo-Adyghean and Semitic language family

**Endonym:** *Аредсис* (Aredan).

**Writing system:** Cyrillic with added letters for specific sounds: Ҟ, Ҕ, Ӷ, Ҩ, Ҭ (Abkhaz). An apostrophe is also used to mark the glottal stop.

**Features:** An agglutinative structure (suffixes are strung together). Verbs have the category of aspect and voice. Word order is SOV (subject-object-verb). There is an ergative construction.

## Additional notes

- The Kenorian language is banned in Bobia; using it is punishable by arrest.
- The Aredan language is also banned in Bobia (as is everything connected with the LKR and Areda).
- In Kenorlandia, Aredan is studied in schools as the second foreign language after English.
- The Bobian language is the only official one in the BPR; all propaganda and documentation is conducted in it.
- All three languages have dialects, but the standard forms are described above.

> **Important:** The languages were created within a fictional world and are not intended to offend real peoples or cultures. All coincidences are accidental.
