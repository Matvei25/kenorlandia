## EBM (Eastern Black Mesa) Substances

| Substance | Description |
|----------|----------|
| **Ununennium** (Uue, 119) | Lives 0.5 seconds – microseconds, decays with high radiation |
| **Unbihexium** (Ubh, 126) | Lives 30 seconds, radioactive, decays into ununennium |
| **Isotopes-Z** | A mix of radioactive substances, liquid, purple color |
| **Foamer** | Airy foam, over time it becomes less dense and less toxic |
| **Ekvey** | A powder substance, light, non-toxic, yellowish (when heated — blue) |
| **Super-Boile gas (SBG)** | Expands 3 times faster, acrid, hot, dense, fast gas |

> EBM substances are used **exclusively** in military and *experimental* cases.
