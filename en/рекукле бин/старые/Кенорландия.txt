Kenorlandia - the Liberal Kenorian Republic (LKR), a country with its capital Kenorlig, with the ideology of democracy and capitalism, official languages English, Russian, Arabic and Kenorian. The currency is the elleyj, it has political instability.

Kenorlandia is currently in a civil war that has been going on for 9 months already.
Allies: Kenorlandia (Kenorian Forces), the USA (military aid + infantry), the Aredan Republic, Lambda-stan.
Axis: Bobia, the DPRK, the Catholic Kenorian State (CKS), the Communist Kenorian Republic (CKR).
Risen Radicals: Vavambik, Yeshubin, Mahalia, the Combine, Turaduna, the Arab Sausage Tribes (not the Combine!).

Kenorlandia is a rich country, but at the same time it's in trouble, it studies teleportation "the new way", creating its "own" - a human analogue of the Combine's technologies, which makes it impossibly important! The CKR is winning for now, Vavambik may fall because of its bad economy. The CKS attacked Mahalia. The Allies and the Opponents come under heavy shelling from the Combine, with mortar synths and plasma missiles. Bobia sometimes starts open war.

The Kenorian language is a mix of Russian, English and Arabic, written in the Latin alphabet, very simple and banned in Bobia!

The Kenorian Forces also took part in an operation with the consent of Israel, Lebanon and Fatah against Hamas and Hezbollah.
