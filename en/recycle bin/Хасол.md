## Khasol State

> Khasol is poorer than Yotagos. Most of the microstate is a village.

Matvey's rival, Amir, created a microstate for friends called Khasol (an ordinary, but larger-scale kids' project, it means nothing to Kenorlandia), where there are more buildings than in Yotagos, here are the main ones:
- **Khasol City Hall** — the first main building, here Amir lives and rules
- **Gidra Department** — the second main building, here the urban citizens of Khasol and the yard kids live
- **Khasolo-Kenorian Internet Cafe** — the third main building, called the Khasolo-office, here Khasol's citizens work and play
- **Khasol Militia** — the fourth zone, called Khasol Forces, here Khasol's soldiers and police live and work
- **Checkpoint "Roa"** — not a building, just a fence and a passage between Khasol and Kenorlandia, it requires a pass
- **Checkpoint "Grek"** — described above
- And another 32 small private houses and/or huts made of cardboard, foam board and construction materials...
