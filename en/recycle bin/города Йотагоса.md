## Borland

- **Borland City Hall** — the main building made of construction materials, it governs the settlement, and the council of elders (elected from each department) also sits here.
- **Shopping arcade "Spectrum"** — a building made of construction materials and scrap metal, 6 small shops: groceries, electronics, souvenirs, clothing, tools, stationery.
- **Plant "Sigma"** — a production building made of construction materials, foam board and scrap metal, it produces spare parts for YBR and drones.
- **Plant "Omega"** — a similar building, it specializes in chemical components for Univerlab (supplies to Yotalig).
- **Ballist-4** and **Ballist-5** — two launchers for YBR, range ≈ 40 km, with compressors and onboard computers.
- **Yota-TV (Borland)** — a micro-building with a broadcast server, it duplicates the `localhost:303` channel, but with local news.
- **Borland Accounting** — tracking of local income and expenses, rental of storage cells.
- **Borland Agency** — a branch of the Agency for finding new premises and candidate residents.
- **Borland Farm** — a 0.2 km² field, they grow potatoes, carrots, greens and strawberries.
- **Warehouse "Accumulator"** — storage for surplus products and construction materials, rented out to outsiders.
- **Barracks "Stronghold"** — a building made of construction materials for the garrison (soldiers, police), with an armory and a training room.
- **Medical post "Doctor"** — a small building made of construction materials, first-aid kit, disinfection, dressing room.
- **Airfield "Roa"** — the second airfield structure, made of construction materials and scrap metal. It includes:
  - A 40 m runway,
  - A tower with a radar and a satellite dish,
  - A hangar for two fixed-wing drones and one quadcopter,
  - An anti-air installation (pneumatic),
  - A command-and-control room with terminals and a server for flight planning.
- Several structures, warehouses, plants, huts, garden beds and micro-buildings made of cardboard, construction materials, scrap metal and foam board.
- Houses with small apartments.
